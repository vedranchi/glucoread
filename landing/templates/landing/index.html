Three kinds of entry. One picture.

Each one takes a moment to record, and each one adds to what the others mean.

Log your readings and see them in context.

Record your glucose through the day and build a history you can actually look back on.

  • Value, in mmol/L or mg/dL
  • Fasting, before meal, after meal, or bedtime
  • An optional note

Keep your doses alongside your readings.

Record insulin doses with the rest of your day, so you don't have to piece your history together later.

  • Basal or bolus
  • Units, and the brand if you want it
  • An optional note

Add the context around your numbers.

Record meals and notes alongside your glucose and insulin to keep a clearer picture of what was happening.

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a snack
  • Carbs, protein, fats, calories — all optional
  • Bread units, at your own grams per unit

Look beyond today.

A single reading tells you what happened once.

Your history shows you what keeps happening.

Every reading stays in your log with its time, its context and where it sat against your range — so three high evenings in a row look like three high evenings, not three unrelated numbers.

The dashboard chart steps back a day at a time, and your glucose log keeps the full week in one table.

Know where your numbers stand.

Set your own upper and lower glucose targets. Every reading is then shown in relation to them — on the log, on the chart, and in the header.

Work in the unit you're comfortable with, mmol/L or mg/dL. Readings are stored once and converted for display, so switching units never changes what you recorded.

Tracking shouldn't get in the way of your day.

  • A reading takes a moment.
  • A meal takes a moment.
  • An insulin dose takes a moment.

One value, when it was taken, and a note if you want one. Nothing is required beyond the number itself, and your target range sits right there on the form so you know where the reading lands.

Your health data belongs to you.

GlucoRead is your own diabetes log. It is not there to sell you anything, and it is not a substitute for your care team.

  • No ads and no trackers

    There is no analytics script, no advertising network and no third-party tag on any page. Your log isn't a marketing asset.

  • Your account, your entries

    Every reading, dose and meal is scoped to your account. Nothing is shared with other users, and there is no shared feed to opt out of.

  • Open source, MIT licensed

    The whole application is public on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does with what you record.

    Read the source

  • A record, not a recommendation

    GlucoRead organises what you record. It doesn't diagnose, dose or advise — treatment decisions stay between you and your care team.
